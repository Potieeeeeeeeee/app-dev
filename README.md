# **Lolo and the Kid**
# **Description**
>lolo and the Kid is a 2024 Filipino drama film about an unlikely duo: an elderly hustler named Lolo (Joel Torre) and a young boy he has taken under his wing. They survive by conning wealthy families, with the boy temporarily "adopted" by them before he steals valuables for them to pawn. The film explores their complex bond, the moral challenges they face, and the boy's desire for a more stable life, which forces Lolo to confront his own fears and his love for the child.

---

# **Details:** 
- **Director:** Benedict Mique 
- **Producer:** Benedict Mique 
- **Release date:** August 7, 2024
- **Genre:** Drama, Family and Education Film.

# **Lesson:** 
> the importance of unconditional family love and intergenerational bonds,  It shows how both the older and younger characters teach and learn from one another, creating a mutually beneficial relationship where wisdom is shared and fresh perspectives are valued
